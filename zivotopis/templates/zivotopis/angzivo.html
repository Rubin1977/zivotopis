ABOUT ME

My Journey Toward Technology

I have always been drawn to precision and systematic thinking — qualities that naturally led me to explore the world of IT. After years of experience as a cytogeneticist in healthcare, I decided to apply this attention to detail to the field of technology. Although my path hasn’t yet taken me into an IT company, I’m driven by a desire to learn and grow — currently preparing for a software testing course.

As a self-learner, I began exploring programming, focusing on the Python language. With the help of the Django Girls tutorial, I created this site as my personal résumé — gaining initial experience in developing web applications using the Django framework and deepening my understanding of HTML and CSS.

Throughout development, I used Visual Studio Code, mastered managing Python virtual environments, and actively worked with Git and GitHub for version control — gaining experience in structured and well-documented development. Later, I expanded into C# and the .NET framework. After completing a dedicated course, I developed a solid understanding of application development within this ecosystem. Visual Studio became my primary tool for development, helping me streamline my work and further enhance my projects.

Alongside this, I spent time abroad accompanying my wife on her diplomatic mission in Brussels. I made the most of this unique life chapter — completing a brewing course at KU Leuven, expanding my technical skills, and building new professional connections. I’m currently working on improving my website rastislavruzbacky.eu.pythonanywhere.com and preparing to continue my IT journey — aiming to find opportunities as a software tester or junior developer.
