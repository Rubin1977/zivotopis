ABOUT ME

My Journey Between Genetics and Technology

I am a cytogeneticist with more than 20 years of experience in clinical genetics in Slovakia. I currently work as a laboratory diagnostician specializing in cytogenetics and aCGH at the Department of Molecular and Biochemical Genetics (Institute of Medical Biology, Genetics and Clinical Genetics, Faculty of Medicine, Comenius University and University Hospital Bratislava), Staré Mesto Hospital.

While living in Brussels, I built on my clinical background by teaching myself programming and data-related skills — Python, Django, C# and .NET, SQL, and QA testing principles. I built this website myself using the Django Girls tutorial, and later expanded into a second web application built with .NET.

Since returning to Slovakia, I actively apply these skills in the laboratory — organizing and structuring laboratory data, digitalizing processes, and approaching quality control (QC) of aCGH and karyotyping results with the same systematic precision that software testing requires.

My goal isn't to leave genetics for IT, but to combine the two: the precision of clinical cytogenetics with the systematic rigor of data work.
